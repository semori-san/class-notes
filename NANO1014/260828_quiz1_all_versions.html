How many in-class activity scores are dropped

The syllabus states that there are no ``make-ups'' for in-class activities. How many in-class activity scores are automatically dropped at the end of the semester?

Correct answer: 2

The five choices were consecutive powers of ten. The exact range, and the order they were listed in, varied between versions.

Why: Two of the lowest in-class activity scores are dropped. Because there are no make-ups for in-class activities, those two drops are what covers you if you have to miss a day.

Getting an absence officially excused

To receive an official excuse for an absence, missed assignment, or missed quiz, the syllabus requires two things. What are they?

Correct answer: Contact Prof. Emori, and provide documentation if required

The other choices were:

  • Contact the TA, and post a note on Canvas
  • Contact the Dean of Students only
  • Contact any one of the additional instructors, and email Prof. Emori afterward
  • Provide a doctor's note, and complete a make-up assignment

Why: Two things are required: contact Prof. Emori, and provide documentation if it is required. Documentation from the Dean of Students office is the gold standard. The most common reason an absence does not end up excused is poor or delayed communication, so the first step matters as much as the paperwork.

Comparisons

Sesame seeds across a human

A human is about 1 m tall. A sesame seed is about 10−3 m wide. If you lined sesame seeds up end to end, about how many would it take to span the height of a human?

Correct answer: 1,000

The five choices were consecutive powers of ten. The exact range, and the order they were listed in, varied between versions.

Why: Subtract the exponents to get the number of steps: 0 minus negative 3 is 3. Three steps means a factor of 10 to the third power, which is 1,000 sesame seeds. Watch the trap: 3 is the number of orders of magnitude, not the factor.

Sesame seed versus sugar molecule

A sesame seed is about 10−3 m wide and a simple sugar molecule is about 10−9 m wide. How many orders of magnitude wider is the sesame seed?

Correct answer: 6

The other choices were:

  • 3
  • 9
  • 1012
  • 106

Why: Subtract the exponents: negative 3 minus negative 9 is 6. So the sesame seed is 6 orders of magnitude wider. The factor would be 10 to the sixth power, which is why 10 to the sixth was on the list as a tempting wrong answer. Orders of magnitude count the steps; the factor is the number you multiply by.

Extra credit

Why the silicon wafer cleaves cleanly

On Monday you tried to cut a shiny ``mystery crystal'' (silicon). A scratch made parallel to the wafer's flat edge cracks cleanly, but a scratch made at a random angle shatters into a jagged mess. Why?

Correct answer: A cut parallel to the flat edge runs between rows of atoms, while a diagonal cut would have to go through them

The other choices were:

  • The wafer is thicker along the diagonal, so more force is needed
  • The crystal is amorphous, so it has no preferred direction of breaking
  • Polishing leaves the surface smoother along the flat edge than across it
  • The flat edge marks where the wafer was sliced from the ingot, so it is already weakened

Why: A crystal is atoms arranged in an orderly, repeating lattice. A scratch parallel to the flat edge lines your cut up with the aisles between rows of atoms, so the wafer splits cleanly along that line. A diagonal cut would have to go straight through the atoms themselves, which you cannot do, so the break jumps from row to row and leaves a jagged edge. That behaviour is indirect evidence that atoms are real and arranged in a discrete lattice.
