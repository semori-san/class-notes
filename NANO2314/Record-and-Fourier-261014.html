    How the screenshot digitizer works: it finds the graticule lines to measure pixels per division, follows the trace one pixel column at a time, and converts pixel positions to seconds and volts. Each pixel column gives one sample, so resolution is one pixel in each direction. Sharp peaks are taken from the edge of the line rather than its centre. Compare the red overlay with the trace before you rely on the numbers.
